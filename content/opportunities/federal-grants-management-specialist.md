The employer describes work contributing federal grants expertise and evaluating AI-generated grants-management outputs.

### Responsibilities

- Draft funding announcements and Notices of Funding Opportunity, including objectives, eligibility, instructions, and evaluation criteria.
- Review and score applications for eligibility, merit, budgets, and alignment with funding requirements.
- Prepare grant-monitoring findings with evidence, regulations, and corrective actions.
- Review and annotate applications, awards, budgets, and compliance reports for training material.
- Evaluate AI outputs for accuracy and compliance, including 2 CFR Part 200.
- Develop scenarios, reference answers, and evaluations across pre-award, award, and post-award work.
