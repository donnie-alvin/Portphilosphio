## Original opportunity description

The employer describes a project evaluating and enhancing AI-generated images and says prior AI experience is not required.

### Responsibilities

- Upload original photographic content through a secure digital platform.
- Compare images with AI-generated counterparts and identify visual flaws or artifacts.
- Document observations about image quality, lighting, composition, perspective, texture, and anomalies.
- Provide clear written feedback and participate in iterative review cycles.
- Collaborate with others and maintain profile information for project tracking.
