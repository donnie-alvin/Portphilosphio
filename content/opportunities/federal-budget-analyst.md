## Original opportunity description

The employer describes work creating realistic federal-budget examples and reviewing AI-generated budget content.

### Responsibilities

- Create training examples of federal budget justifications, narratives, exhibits, and supporting analysis.
- Write sample OMB passback responses and annotate reasoning.
- Document mid-year reprogramming scenarios and approval requirements.
- Compare expenditures with planned obligations, assess fund availability, and explain variances.
- Walk through year-end closeout, including reconciliation and discrepancy resolution.
- Write guidance on funding types, budget changes, and compliance.
- Produce templates, walkthroughs, and annotated examples.
- Review AI-generated content for accuracy, realism, and compliance.
