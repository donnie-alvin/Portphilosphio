## Original opportunity description

The employer says the project uses medical-coding expertise to produce annotations and feedback for AI systems.

### Responsibilities

- Annotate and review medical records and assign ICD-10-CM, ICD-10-PCS, CPT, and HCPCS codes based on documentation.
- Identify and sequence diagnoses; assign modifiers and POA indicators where applicable.
- Apply coding conventions and official guidance; flag documentation gaps and query opportunities.
- Resolve ambiguities with stakeholders.
- Provide feedback on coding guidelines and complex cases.
- Ensure annotations are accurate and complete.
