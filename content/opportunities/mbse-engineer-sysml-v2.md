## Original opportunity description

The employer seeks professionals with SysML v1 expertise and SysML v2 exposure, or hands-on SysML v2 textual modeling experience, to build reference models, assess generated models, and define quality standards.

### Responsibilities

- Translate engineering requirements into SysML v2 textual models with fidelity and traceability.
- Develop reference solutions and exemplar architectures.
- Create and refine evaluation rubrics.
- Review generated models for technical accuracy and modeling best practices.
- Analyze requirements, ConOps, ICDs, and architecture documents.
- Collaborate asynchronously to clarify deliverables and technical questions.

### Required qualifications and skills

- Professional systems-engineering experience, with hands-on SysML v1 and some SysML v2 exposure, or demonstrated SysML v2 textual modeling experience.
- Ability to translate engineering requirements into accurate system models.
- Understanding of MBSE, requirements traceability, and system architecture.

### Preferred qualifications

The listing prefers requirements engineering, interface control, model traceability, ConOps/ICD work, technical writing, complex-industry experience, model verification or review, rubric development, and AI/ML exposure.
