## Original opportunity description

The employer says the project uses chart-review and coding expertise to evaluate records and provide feedback for AI systems.

### Responsibilities

- Review medical records, including admission and progress notes, operative reports, consults, lab and imaging results, and discharge summaries.
- Validate diagnosis and procedure coding, sequencing, POA indicators, and MS-DRG assignments against documentation.
- Apply ICD-10-CM/PCS guidance, AHA Coding Clinic guidance, and clinical validation criteria; flag gaps and query opportunities.
- Clarify requirements with stakeholders and help maintain data integrity.
- Provide feedback on charts, coding guidance, and complex cases.
- Ensure review findings are accurate and complete.
