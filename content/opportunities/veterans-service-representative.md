## Original opportunity description

The employer describes work applying real-world veterans-claims expertise to AI outputs and training examples.

### Responsibilities

- Review AI-generated disability-compensation claims and evaluate evidence against regulatory guidance.
- Assess medical documentation and its relevance to existing claims.
- Make benefit determinations and document rationales.
- Quality-check AI-generated decisions against VA standards.
- Use VBMS or similar claims systems to manage claim files.
- Simulate higher-level review decisions for appeals and supplemental claims.
- Provide written and verbal feedback.
