## Original opportunity description

The employer says the project uses borrower-counseling expertise to check whether questions and answers reflect real borrower situations and current practices.

### Responsibilities

- Review sample federal student-loan questions and flag unrealistic borrower inquiries.
- Draft and refine rubrics for accurate answers about repayment, forgiveness, and relief programs.
- Cover loan servicing, deferment, forbearance, and default-management scenarios.
- Evaluate AI-generated answers for inaccuracies, missing detail, and outdated guidance.
- Provide concise asynchronous feedback and flag incorrect guidance or problematic servicing advice.
- Collaborate with other experts to keep guidance aligned with current practices.
